A tap or other input on a control behind an open sheet or app-modal window fails with `ACTION_FAILED` and sends nothing. A tap, `check`, `uncheck`, or `scrollIntoView` scrolls a control out of view into view by its scroll area's scroll bar when the control has no scroll action of its own. A tap whose handler blocks the app for a few seconds, or quits it, succeeds. Names no longer carry the invisible bidirectional marks AppKit adds, and ids AppKit makes up (`_NS:61`, a menu item's `chose:`) are no test ids. `e2e doctor` checks that `app.bundleId` names an installed app. The native driver refuses to load when it is older than its sources in a checkout, or when the platform package's version differs from the engine's.
