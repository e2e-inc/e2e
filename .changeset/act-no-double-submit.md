The built-in agent's rules (`BASE_RULES`) now tell the model not to press a save or submit control again when the first press shows no change or is still in progress. The model must first check the screen for a confirmation, an error, a new location, or the changed record. It presses again only when that check shows the first press did not commit, or when the tap only closed the on-screen keyboard, and does not press again when it cannot tell. A step that asks for several commits still presses once for each. It also stays on the step's screen instead of navigating away to confirm a save, so a save that shows no confirmation on that screen can end the step `failed`; name the check in the step ("save the product, then confirm it appears in the product list"). The loop guard gives the built-in `observe` more room while a save is still in progress: identical observe calls now warn at six and force a verdict at ten, where every other tool keeps three and five, so such a step can spend up to five more model calls.
