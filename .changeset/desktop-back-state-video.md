The desktop engine adds `app.back()`, which presses the app's Back menu item (one titled Back or Go Back, else the one whose shortcut is Command-[, which an app may give another command) and fails when the app has neither, and `app.clearState()` and `test.setup` sessions for an `app.appPath` target: a reset clears the app's defaults, saved window state, and container, and refuses while macOS knows another copy of the bundle id. `video: 'on'`, `--video on`, and `app.startRecording()` record the app's window with ScreenCaptureKit (macOS 15 or later, with Screen Recording permission), one file per window `screen` reads. Rows a list holds out of view read as one hidden line, so a long list reads as fast as a short one; `scrollUntilVisible` brings a row in.
